## What affects the higher rubric bands

The rubric rewards evidence, not just the presence of pages. Add real audience research and task observations, explain how findings changed copy/navigation, and include genuine group contribution records. Animation supports the creative/interactivity criteria, but does not by itself prove intuitive navigation, research depth or increased sales.

## Items that are not explicit requirements

Student IDs, analytics installation, a backend enquiry service and on-site payment are not expressly required by the supplied brief. They should not be presented as mandatory rubric items. JavaScript is not expressly prohibited, but the brief's HTML/CSS wording may warrant lecturer clarification if a stricter rule was given separately.
