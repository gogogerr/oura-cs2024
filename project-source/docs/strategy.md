# Proposed marketing and measurement plan

## Positioning

Form / Function presents Oura Ring 4 as a discreet way to explore everyday wellness patterns. The audience is a hypothesis: Singapore adults interested in sleep and recovery who prefer a screenless wearable. Test that hypothesis through voluntary interviews and task-based usability sessions before treating it as research evidence.

## Journey and SEO

Social product rotations introduce the object, while search content answers specific buying questions. Suggested search themes: Oura Ring 4 sizing Singapore, Oura membership cost, smart ring sleep tracking and Oura Ring 4 titanium finishes. Each relevant page answers a distinct question and leads toward choosing a size and checking a retailer. Avoid promises to diagnose conditions or improve sleep outcomes.

## Three-month campaign proposal

The campaign runs for three calendar months after the agreed launch date. Preparation occurs before launch.

1. Before launch: agree seller cooperation and order attribution, obtain a comparable three-month baseline, verify stock/warranty and observe voluntary target-user tasks. Do not record health information.
2. Month 1: publish short product demonstrations and original sleep/sizing articles. Use distinct campaign parameters on inbound links. Begin with organic content; a paid budget requires a separate decision.
3. Month 2: compare benefits-first versus sizing-first CTA order with comparable traffic. Review where visitors struggle, then improve copy and navigation. Do not declare a winner from a small or biased sample.
4. Month 3: extend the content that helps buying decisions, address weak funnel steps and evaluate seller-confirmed completed orders against the agreed baseline.

## Sales objective

Target a 20% relative increase in completed sales within three calendar months after campaign launch, compared with a comparable three-month baseline. Formula: `(campaign sales − comparable baseline sales) / comparable baseline sales × 100`. A purely hypothetical baseline of 100 orders would imply a target of 120 orders. These are illustrative numbers, not sales data. A zero baseline cannot be used in this formula.

Compare matched periods and channel mix, using a control group or holdout when feasible. Document promotions, stockouts, price changes and seasonality. The site cannot observe external checkout completion without seller cooperation. Outbound clicks are purchase-intent indicators, not sales.

## Funnel instrumentation proposal

After a suitable privacy review, measure finish selection, sizing-guide visits, retailer CTA clicks and genuine enquiry completion. Define engagement as sessions using a meaningful control divided by eligible sessions. Define retailer click-through as sessions with an outbound purchase click divided by eligible sessions. Report actual orders from the seller separately. No analytics is currently installed, so none of these metrics is being collected.

## Evidence and limits

Oura's product and support pages substantiate the technical copy. Shopee search indexing establishes a marketplace listing only. No seller partnership, customer testimonial, clinical evidence of benefit, achieved uplift or measured usability result is claimed. Source URLs and dates are on the site's research page. Check current stock, membership availability and terms before publication or purchase.
