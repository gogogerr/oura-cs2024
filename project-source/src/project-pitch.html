02 / 18 — CS2024

The goal is 20% sales growth
within three months

Proposed measure: seller-confirmed completed orders during the three calendar months after campaign launch, compared with a comparable three-month baseline.

Growth = (campaign orders − baseline orders) / baseline orders × 100.
This is a target. No sales uplift has been observed or promised.

03 / 18 — CS2024

Design thinking guides
the buying journey

Empathise: desk research suggests three needs: sleep context, recovery and everyday activity.
Define: visitors need relevance, evidence, fit and ownership costs.
Ideate: persona routes converge on one buying-guide form.
Prototype: nine core pages plus five supporting pages.
Test: browser checks completed; physical-phone and target-user testing remain open.

06 / 18 — CS2024

Creative brief
Form / Function

Promise: help visitors decide whether Ring 4 fits their routine.
Tone: clear, precise and lightly playful.
Concept: the ring travels from a work desk through sensing to finish selection.
Primary conversion: create a personalised buying guide, then continue to the exact Shopee listing.
Campaign: S$20 voucher and sizing-kit support are proposals only, subject to retailer approval.

07 / 18 — CS2024

Font selection

Arial / Helvetica: bold display headlines and clear body copy. Familiar system fonts load without external requests.
Georgia: editorial emphasis in feature and supporting-page headings.
Monospace system fallback: scene labels and technical captions.
Hierarchy: oversized short headings, readable body copy, compact secondary labels. Important buying information stays in body text.

14 / 18 — CS2024

Content strategy
Answers before persuasion

Relevant feature pages answer each persona’s main objection.
Sizing and membership copy explain practical buying decisions before the product link.
The free personalised guide is the working incentive; campaign benefits are labelled proposals.
Availability is a launch gate: verify an orderable size, seller terms and fulfilment before promoting purchases.
Validate persona assumptions with observed tasks; no conversion uplift is claimed.

16 / 18 — CS2024

Buying guide and privacy

Choose Sleep, Recovery or Activity and optional name, finish and size. No email field.
Required local-processing consent and separate optional future-updates preference are unticked.
The guide appears on-page and can be downloaded as text; the matching Shopee link follows the selected finish.
No data is submitted, no mailing subscription is created and no payment is taken. Clear the result to withdraw this local choice.
